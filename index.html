Welcome Message

The International Cruise Conference & Symposium (ICCS) is the premier forum for the presentation of new advances and research results in the fields of maritime engineering and intelligent systems.

This year, we are proud to host the event on the 'Ocean Dream', fostering a unique environment for networking and collaboration amidst the serene backdrop of the East Sea.

Paper Submission

Share your research with the global community.

Topics of Interest

We invite submissions on a wide range of topics, including but not limited to:

  • Smart Ship Technology
  • Marine AI & Big Data
  • Autonomous Navigation
  • Sustainable Ocean Tourism
  • Port Logistics
  • Human-Robot Interaction

Submission Guidelines

1. Format: Papers must be written in English and formatted according to the IEEE standard template.

2. Length: Regular papers should be 4-6 pages. Short papers 2-4 pages.

3. Review: All submissions will be peer-reviewed by at least two independent reviewers.
